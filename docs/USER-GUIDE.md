# Reel Studio — User Guide

Reel Studio is a free, all-in-one faceless reel maker that runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no API keys, no paid services.

## Quick start (Auto mode)

1. Open `index.html` (or your hosted link).
2. Pick a **niche** (Tech, Politics, Relationships, Money, Motivation).
3. Pick a **format** (3 Things, Myth vs Reality, POV, Did You Know, Checklist, Before/After, Unpopular Opinion, Explained Simply…).
4. Pick a **length** — presets from 15 seconds to 10 minutes, or type a custom duration in seconds or minutes.
5. Type a **topic** — or tap any day in the built-in 30-day content library.
6. Hit generate. Reel Studio writes a hook-first script, a caption + hashtag pack, finds topic-relevant pictures and videos by itself, and renders the video.

## What it generates

- **Script** — hook in the first 2 seconds, substantive beats, a save line, and a call-to-action ending. Written in a natural human tone.
- **Voiceover script** — the full speakable version of the script (longer than the on-screen captions), in its own panel with copy/rebuild buttons.
- **Caption + hashtags** — written in posting style (ends with a question or call to comment), with a copy button.
- **Media credits** — copy-ready credit lines for the stock media used, for your caption.

## Media

- **Automatic** — every scene searches Wikimedia Commons for photos and videos matching that scene's keywords. Videos are preferred; each scene gets a different clip; the old generic image pack is only a last resort.
- **Manual search** — every scene has a 🔍 Find button: search anything, browse thumbnails (videos show duration badges), tap to assign.
- **Re-fetch** — ⟳ on a scene, or "Re-fetch topic media for all scenes" to roll new media everywhere.
- **Your own files** — upload images/video clips per scene, drag-and-drop onto any scene, or bulk-upload clips to spread across scenes. Your uploads always win and are never overwritten.
- **Preference** — Videos first / Photos first in the Auto panel.
- **Optional Pexels key** — paste a free Pexels API key in settings for higher-end stock; it's stored only in your browser and sent only to api.pexels.com. Without it, everything works via Commons.

## Voiceover

Step 4, "Voiceover", one click:

- **Generate voiceover** — uses your browser's built-in voices (free, no keys). Pick a voice, adjust speed and pitch. Your browser will ask permission to capture tab audio once; scenes auto-time to the narration and the voice is mixed into the exported video.
- **Record with mic** or **upload an audio file** instead.
- **Premium option** — a button opens the Free AI Voiceover Studio; generate a higher-quality AI voice there, download the file, upload it back here.

## Formats and export

- **Reel 9:16** (1080×1920 or 720×1280) for Instagram / Facebook / TikTok.
- **YouTube 16:9** (1920×1080 or 1280×720) for YouTube.
- **Render my reel** renders in real time — a 60-second reel takes 60 seconds; keep the tab open. The download button then gives you the video file (WebM in Chrome, MP4 in Safari; YouTube accepts WebM directly, Instagram prefers MP4).
- Keep on-screen text clear of the bottom ~250px and the right edge — platform UI overlays live there.

## Engagement features (always on)

Giant pattern-interrupt hook scene, word-by-word karaoke captions with keyword emphasis, Ken Burns zoom/pan, zoom-cut transitions, bottom progress bar, loopable endings, niche CTAs, safe-zone-aware layout.

## Tips

- Post daily 7–9pm PKT for best reach (per the built-in calendar notes).
- Faceless, neutral-politics, no-religion defaults are baked into the generator.
- Longer reels (3–10 min) work best for YouTube; 15–30s for Reels/Shorts/TikTok.
- If Commons results look thin for a topic, add your free Pexels key or upload your own clips.

## Privacy

Everything runs locally in your browser. The only network calls are media searches (Wikimedia Commons, and Pexels only if you add a key) and the optional Voiceover Studio link. Nothing you type or upload leaves your device otherwise.
